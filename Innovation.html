01
VBA · Excel · Process improvement

Civil Lab Data Automation

THE FRICTION

Problem

Manual data logging and repeated template preparation created avoidable administrative work in a civil engineering laboratory.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Solution

I developed a VBA-based tool that generates pre-filled templates from a central input sheet, reducing repetitive copying and supporting more consistent data capture.

PROCESS VALUE

Outcome

An example of applying Lean thinking within an existing toolset rather than requiring a complete system replacement.

02
Inventory · Digital records · Data visibility

Composite Engineering Inventory

THE FRICTION

Problem

Paper-based material logs made it harder to trace movements and understand stock consumption.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Solution

I developed a digital inventory tracking system to capture material movements and provide a clearer basis for reviewing stock and procurement needs.

PROCESS VALUE

Outcome

Structured records support stock visibility and analysis; data accuracy still depends on consistent operational inputs.

03
Power BI · Excel · DAX · DMAIC

Farm Mania

THE FRICTION

Problem

Disconnected poultry stock and sales records made timely operational reporting difficult.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Solution

I developed a Power BI dashboard pilot to bring inventory and sales into a shared data model and make stock and revenue patterns easier to interpret.

PROCESS VALUE

Outcome

The pilot highlighted that standardised data collection and stronger SOPs are essential before scaling a reporting solution.
