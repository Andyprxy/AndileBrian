THE OPERATIONAL CONTEXT

When inventory
is a living system.

This pilot explored how a business intelligence decision-support system could combine fragmented records into a useful view of poultry inventory. It demonstrated the value of a connected data model and exposed a more fundamental requirement: consistent data capture. Waste reduction and feed-conversion improvements remain opportunities to validate, rather than measured outcomes of this pilot.

ENGINEERING THE IMPROVEMENT

Five phases.
One connected process.

Define the need. Measure the baseline. Understand the cause. Test the solution. Prepare to sustain it.

01Define02Measure03Analyse04Improve05Control
01

Define

Frame the operational problem.

Poultry inventory is biological: it grows, consumes feed and changes daily. Manual stock logs and a separate point-of-sale record made it difficult to see how coop activity translated into available stock and revenue.

The pilot question
Can biological inventory data be brought together into a useful, current view for operational decisions?

The problem

Stock movements, mortality and sales were recorded in disconnected places.

The objective

Build a prototype dashboard to connect those records and assess feasibility.

02

Measure

Establish the starting point.

The baseline review examined how information was captured, stored and passed to management. Two constraints stood out.

3–4 days

Reported baseline delay before information reached management.

Split records

Digital sales data alongside mortality records captured on paper.

03

Analyse

Find the constraint behind the data.

Bringing the records together exposed data-quality issues that were less visible in the manual process. The key bottleneck was the consistency of data capture at coop level, rather than the dashboard logic alone.

Reliable insight starts with reliable inputs. The operating procedure needed the same attention as the software.

That finding changed the improvement priority: establish standard data inputs and clearer operating procedures before expanding the system.

04

Improve

Make the proposed solution tangible.

A Power BI decision-support pilot brought the separate data streams into a unified model, with dynamic visuals and DAX-based logic to explore inventory behaviour.

05

Control

Prepare for reliable operation.

The pilot concluded with a feasibility report and a roadmap for future readiness. The recommendation was to standardise source inputs through operating procedures before scaling.

  • 01Standardise capture. Make the required inputs and recording procedure consistent.
  • 02Protect data quality. Review the reliability of source records before relying on dashboard outputs.
  • 03Scale when ready. Use the feasibility findings to guide the next implementation stage.
THE ENGINEERING TAKEAWAY

A dashboard makes a process visible. Consistent operating practices make that visibility trustworthy.
